Under **Prof. Joseph L. Moore**, Johns Hopkins University.

- Developed a hybrid control system for a 2-link hopper, using **neural network dynamics residuals** to model complex contact forces that analytic models miss.
- Used **Proximal Policy Optimization** to generate trajectory data and train the residuals, improving simulation accuracy and balance.
- Integrated the hybrid dynamics model with a **Model Predictive Path Integral (MPPI)** controller for real-time control.
