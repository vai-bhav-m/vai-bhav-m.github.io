Under **Prof. Enrique Mallada**, Johns Hopkins University.

- Developed and trained a **UR5e** robotic arm in the **Mujoco** simulation environment using OpenAI's Stable-Baselines3.
- Designed state and action spaces and implemented reward shaping to optimize training across multiple manipulator agents.
- Benchmarked real-time performance of agents trained with four RL algorithms: **SAC, A2C, TD3 and PPO**.
