Hackathon under **Prof. Kaushik Mitra**, IIT Madras. **Won 3rd place** among 150+
participants across 45+ teams.

- Modeled atmospheric haze and executed a state-of-the-art de-hazing algorithm to enhance low-light mosquito images.
- Trained a novel ensemble of **YOLOv8** for detection with **Inception V3** for classification, obtaining a **mAP of 61.3%**.
