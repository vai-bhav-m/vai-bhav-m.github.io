Under **Prof. Jerry L. Prince**, Johns Hopkins University.

- Trained a **UNet** for automated left ventricular segmentation from echocardiogram videos across **7,460 patient recordings**.
- Engineered an empirical **area-to-volume relationship** to estimate ejection fraction, achieving an **RMSE of 1.42%**.
- Secured the best EF estimation performance among 40+ students under rigorous evaluation.
